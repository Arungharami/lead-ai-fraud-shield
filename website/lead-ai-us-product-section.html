How It Works

1

Transaction Arrives

Your checkout, payment gateway, or CRM sends transaction data to the API.

2

Model Scores It

GBM model evaluates 9 risk features and assigns a probability score in milliseconds.

3

SHAP Explains Why

SHAP TreeExplainer identifies the top risk drivers and generates a plain-English summary.

4

Your Team Acts

Route Low Risk to auto-approve. Send High Risk to your analyst queue with full context attached.
